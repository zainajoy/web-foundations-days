About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple application for creating and keeping track of notes. It helps users quickly write down important ideas, tasks, and reminders.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Enter your note in the New note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. View your saved notes in the Your notes section.

Features

  • Create new notes quickly.
  • View your saved notes.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.
  • Keep important ideas and reminders in one place.
